# CatanX photoreal terrain set v2

The five v2 material families are original CatanX assets generated or
reprocessed on 2026-07-22: meadow, wheat stubble, alpine rock, red clay and
dune sand. Each family contains albedo, tangent-space normal, roughness, AO and
height maps at 2048 px.

The generated prompts requested orthographic seamless material scans with
neutral cross-polarized lighting, no objects, no text, no watermark and no
baked directional shadows. Companion channels were produced deterministically
with `tools/build_pbr_from_albedo.py`.
