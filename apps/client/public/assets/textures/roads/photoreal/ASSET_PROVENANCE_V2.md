# Dirt road material v2

- Source: generated specifically for CatanX with OpenAI image generation.
- Date: 2026-07-22.
- Prompt direction: seamless orthographic pale compacted European country road, fine gravel and subtle twin wheel ruts, neutral cross-polarized light, no baked shadows or borders.
- Processing: CatanX local PBR derivation pipeline (`tools/build_pbr_from_albedo.py`) at 2048 px.
- Channels: albedo, normal, roughness, ambient occlusion and height.
- Intended use: natural Surface roads in the Diorama renderer.
