# Rural road material v3

- Source: original CatanX asset generated with the built-in OpenAI image workflow.
- Date: 2026-07-22.
- Art direction: orthographic compacted medieval rural earth, fine gravel,
  restrained grass margins and two continuous wheel ruts.
- Constraints: neutral diffuse light, no objects, no baked directional shadow,
  no watermark and no dominant unique landmark.
- Processing: deterministic 2048 px normal, roughness, AO and height derivation
  through `tools/build_pbr_from_albedo.py`.
- Runtime use: natural topology roads and the Diorama reference scene.
